Process

How I work

A fast, AI-powered workflow. You share the idea, and you see a real, working website quickly.

  1. Step 1

    Brief

    Tell me about your business and what the site needs to do. A short chat or a few messages is enough to start.

  2. Step 2

    Build with AI

    I use AI-powered design tools to turn your brief into a working website fast, often in days rather than weeks.

  3. Step 3

    Refine

    You review it live in your browser, and I adjust the layout, words and effects until it feels right.

  4. Step 4

    Launch

    Your site goes live on your domain, checked for speed, mobile layout and search visibility.
